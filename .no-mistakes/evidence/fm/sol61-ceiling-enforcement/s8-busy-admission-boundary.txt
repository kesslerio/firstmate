### S8 (adversarial): the admission boundary stays busy while an uncapped replacement runs

--- starting state
$ cat config/sol-cap
max=2
models=gpt-6-sol
$ cat state/sol-sessions.tsv
task-r	gpt-6.1-sol	1793451613	-1
holder alive as pid 2049806

--- the boundary is held by a live process:
state/.sol-cap.lock/pid = 2049806 (live: yes)

$ bin/fm-sol-cap-lib.sh refuse-claim task-r ship codex gpt-6.1-sol $FM_HOME/config $FM_HOME/state   # the uncapped replacement
not-capped
exit=0

--- while the boundary was still held:
published invalidation:      .sol-cap-supersede-task-r
anything else matching it:   1
task-r row pid (unchanged):  -1
boundary still held:         yes

--- release the boundary and restore a declaration that covers gpt-6.1-sol
publishing marker still present: .sol-cap-supersede-task-r

$ bin/fm-sol-cap-lib.sh refuse-claim task-s ship codex gpt-6.1-sol $FM_HOME/config $FM_HOME/state   # the next admission
claimed
exit=0

--- after the next admission:
task-r row pid (superseded, claims no slot until adoption): -2
task-s row model (its claim survived): gpt-6.1-sol
invalidations left:                                      none
$ cat state/sol-sessions.tsv
task-r	gpt-6.1-sol	1793451615	-2
task-s	gpt-6.1-sol	1793451615	2053662

--- now a live capped session occupies the other slot:
$ bin/fm-sol-cap-lib.sh refuse-claim task-l ...
claimed
$ bin/fm-sol-cap-lib.sh run task-l $FM_HOME/state sleep 300   (the reservation moves to the worker)
task-l row pid 2054025 -> sleep 300 (live: yes)
$ cat state/sol-sessions.tsv
task-r	gpt-6.1-sol	1793451615	-2
task-l	gpt-6.1-sol	1793451615	2054025

$ bin/fm-sol-cap-lib.sh refuse-claim task-x ship codex gpt-6.1-sol $FM_HOME/config $FM_HOME/state   # above the ceiling
error: Sol ceiling reached: 2 concurrent capped session(s) already active and this ceiling is 2 (declared in config/sol-cap); the dispatch of task task-x (codex, gpt-6.1-sol) was refused. Active sessions: task-l (gpt-6.1-sol, age 0s); task-r (gpt-6.1-sol, age 0s). A session frees its slot when its worker's process ends; the dispatch contract refuses at the ceiling instead of queueing
