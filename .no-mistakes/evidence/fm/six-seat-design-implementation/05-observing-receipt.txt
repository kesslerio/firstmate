Scenario: a launch that found a supervisor already running on another model is recognized by
the receipt that observed it, so the requested model never takes a seat and enabling pools
does not block pooled launches fleet-wide.

Lab: remote $LAB/remote2 served by the primary. The host holds the durable records such a
launch leaves behind (the documented host-side contract of
bin/fm-remote-secondmate-control.sh):
  state/parent-route/obs.meta                     model=model-b spawn_gen=obs-gen
  state/parent-route/obs.seat-operation.op-obs    actual_generation=obs-gen actual_model=model-b
  state/parent-route/obs.seat-reservation.op-obs  immutable holder, model=model-a (the model asked for)
The parent's ledger holds the reserved candidate for operation op-obs on model-a.
Both models are in the one declared pool, as they would be for one shared endpoint.

# positive: the receipt proves the generation and model that run, so the serve completes and
# the supervisor is parent-accounted instead of adopted a second time:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=1827240823-89 epoch=r17935999991714872872.11 holders=1
