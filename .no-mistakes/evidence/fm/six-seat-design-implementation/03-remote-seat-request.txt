Scenario: a pooled launch filed from a remote secondmate home waits for the primary's next
serve and is refused when the shared pool is full, and granted when there is capacity; the
granted generation is then counted at the root.

Lab: as in 02, capacity 2 (mate1 + the remote's own supervisor ios = full).

# the remote home files its seat request and waits:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/remote bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-r --generation g-r1 --kind ship \
    --harness claude --model pool-model-a --holder-pid <live pid>   &
# the primary's watcher pass answers it:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=3130302336-69 epoch=r17935999991714872872.4 holders=1
fleet-seats: pool shared is full (2 of 2 seats held); task-r gets no seat for model pool-model-a - choose an overflow route or wait for a holder to finish
remote-reserve rc=4

# capacity raised to 3, served, then the same remote request is granted by the root:
$ (capacity 3) FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=4092903195-69 epoch=r17935999991714872872.5 holders=1
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/remote bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-r --generation g-r1 ... &
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=4092903195-69 epoch=r17935999991714872872.6 holders=2
fleet-seats: reserved pool=shared id=task-r generation=g-r1 (granted by the fleet root)
remote-reserve rc=0

# the granted remote generation is part of the root's shared capacity:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-x --generation g-x1 ...
fleet-seats: pool shared is full (3 of 3 seats held); task-x gets no seat for model pool-model-a - choose an overflow route or wait for a holder to finish
  holder remote:ios:$LAB/remote/state task-r
  holder $LAB/mate1/state mate1
  holder $LAB/primary/state ios
[exit 4]
