Scenario: a supervisor already running on a registered remote host before pools existed is
counted exactly once after pools are enabled, so enabling pools around it does not spend the
fleet's capacity twice and does not block pooled launches.

Lab: primary $LAB/primary; remote $LAB/remote registered in the primary's
data/secondmates.md (host: localhost; root: <gate worktree>; home: $LAB/remote) and reached
through bin/fm-on.sh over real SSH. The remote home records its pre-pool supervisor in
state/parent-route/ios.meta (model=pool-model-a, spawn_gen=gen-ios). The primary's own route
record state/ios.meta predates generation recording (no remote_spawn_gen/fleet_seat_generation).
The local secondmate mate1 also holds one seat.

$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=3130302336-69 epoch=r17935999991714872872.1 holders=1

$ jq . $LAB/primary/state/fleet-seats/remote-ios.cert
{"schema":"fm-fleet-seats-serve.v2","policy_digest":"3130302336-69","epoch":"r17935999991714872872.1",
 "complete":true,
 "holders":[{"state_dir":"$LAB/remote/state/parent-route","task":"ios","generation":"gen-ios",
             "model":"pool-model-a","lifecycle":"confirmed"}]}

# capacity 2 = mate1 + the adopted remote supervisor. It refuses at exactly 2, not 3:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-b --generation g-b1 ...
fleet-seats: pool shared is full (2 of 2 seats held); task-b gets no seat for model pool-model-a - choose an overflow route or wait for a holder to finish
  holder $LAB/mate1/state mate1
  holder $LAB/primary/state ios
[exit 4]

# raising the declaration to 3 invalidates the certificate, and the product refuses until
# the remote confirms the new declaration (no launch without a counted seat):
$ (capacity 3) FM_HOME=$LAB/primary bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-b ...
fleet-seats: no seat - remote secondmate(s) ios have not confirmed the current seat policy, so their agents cannot be counted yet (a pooled model is never launched without a counted seat; choose another route or repair the authority)
[exit 5]

# after the next serve the same reservation is admitted at 3 of 3: the adopted supervisor
# is one seat, and enabling pools around it did not block the launch:
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary FM_SSH_BIN=<lab ssh> bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh serve-remotes
served ios policy=4092903195-69 epoch=r17935999991714872872.2 holders=1
$ FM_HOME=$LAB/primary bin/fm-fleet-seats.sh reserve task-b --generation g-b1 ...
fleet-seats: reserved pool=shared id=task-b generation=g-b1 used=3 capacity=3
[exit 0]
